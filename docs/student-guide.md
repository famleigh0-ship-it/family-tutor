# Your Study App — Quick Guide

## Getting started

- The app is at **https://family-tutor-ten.vercel.app**
- Add it to your home screen so it opens like a real app:
  1. Open the link in **Safari** (has to be Safari, not Chrome, for this to work on iPhone)
  2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom toolbar)
  3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
  4. Tap **Add**
  5. Open it from the new icon on your home screen from now on — no browser bar around it, just the app
- Your login: **s.a.clayton144@gmail.com** (ask Dad for the password)

## Daily routine (15-30 min)

1. Open the app
2. Log what you covered in class today (photo of notes, or just type it)
3. Start your session — the app picks what to practice based on what you've learned and what needs work
4. Answer questions, read the feedback, move to the next one
5. Done — come back tomorrow

## Before a quiz or test

- Tap **"Quiz coming up?"** on the course card on your home screen
- Pick the topics the quiz covers
- Set the quiz date (up to 30 days out)
- Your next sessions will focus on those topics until the quiz
- After the quiz, the app will ask how it went — answer honestly, it adjusts your practice either way

## Logging class notes

Three ways — use whichever's easiest that day:

- 📷 **Photo**: take a pic of your notes or the board
- ✏️ **Text**: type a quick description (e.g. "Newton's third law and free body diagrams")
- ☑️ **Checklist**: tap topics from a list, no typing needed

## If something goes wrong

- **App not loading**: check your connection, try closing and reopening it
- **Forgot password**: tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen
- **Question seems wrong**: it happens occasionally with generated questions — skip it, it won't count against you, and mention it to Dad so he can fix it for next time
- **App shows "Something went wrong"**: tap Reload — your progress is saved, this won't lose anything
- **Anything else**: ask Dad

## Tips

- Darker ink + good lighting = better photo grading
- Write out your reasoning on free-response questions — a right answer with no explanation scores zero on the real AP exam too, so it's worth practicing that way now
- The app tracks your streak (🔥 on your home screen) — try to keep it going, even a short session counts
- Early in the year you'll only see a topic or two unlocked per course — that's normal, more unlocks as the year goes on and as you log what's covered in class
